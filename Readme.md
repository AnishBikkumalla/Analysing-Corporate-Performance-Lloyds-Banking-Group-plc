# Analysing Corporate Performance: Lloyds Banking Group plc

This repository contains a comprehensive financial analysis of Lloyds Banking Group plc (LBG) spanning the years 2023 to 2025[cite: 3]. Prepared from the perspective of a financial consultancy advising a prospective equity investor, the project evaluates the bank's corporate governance, financial statements, working capital management, and overall capital structure[cite: 3]. 

## Repository Contents

*   **`Anish.docx`**: The primary research report detailing the strategic and financial analysis, complete with executive summaries, governance reviews, and final investment recommendations[cite: 3].
*   **`Anish.xlsx`**: The companion Excel workbook containing the raw financial data, trend analysis models, and comprehensive ratio calculations used to benchmark LBG against industry peers[cite: 3].

## Key Highlights & Findings

### 1. Financial Performance and Profitability
*   After a dip in 2024 due to net interest margin (NIM) compression, LBG's Net Interest Income (NII) recovered to £13,230 million in 2025, driven by the reinvestment of structural hedges at higher prevailing rates[cite: 3]. 
*   Total income grew to £19,422 million in 2025, pushing Profit Before Tax (PBT) up 12% to £6,661 million[cite: 3].
*   The Return on Tangible Equity (RoTE) reached 12.9% in 2025, successfully outperforming the peer average of 11.8%[cite: 3].

### 2. Capital Strength and Solvency
*   The bank maintains a robust Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio of 14.0%, which comfortably exceeds the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) minimum requirement of approximately 12%[cite: 3].
*   LBG's Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) stands at 145%, and the Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR) sits conservatively at 97%, indicating a highly stable, deposit-driven funding model[cite: 3].

### 3. Corporate Governance
*   LBG is in full compliance with the UK Corporate Governance Code 2018[cite: 3].
*   The Board features strong diversity metrics, including 50% female representation and 20% ethnic minority representation, exceeding FTSE Women Leaders targets[cite: 3]. 
*   Executive compensation is closely aligned with long-term shareholder value, contributing to a massive 87.9% total shareholder return in 2025[cite: 3].

### 4. Investment Recommendation
*   **Recommendation: BUY**[cite: 3]. LBG is recommended for income-oriented investors due to its strong capital generation and progressive distribution policies[cite: 3].
*   Dividends per share have grown 15% year-over-year, reaching 3.65 pence in 2025, supplemented by an ongoing £1.71 billion share buyback program[cite: 3].
*   **Primary Risk Factor:** The most significant near-term risk is an unresolved motor finance liability (£1,950 million provisioned at year-end 2025), which awaits final Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) scheme rules expected in May 2026[cite: 3].
